zk-HyperKVM Workbench

Inspect binaries and generate proofs on this device. A content security policy blocks this page from sending anything over the network, and everything runs in WebAssembly.

Runs a guest program under the verifiable hypervisor, folds every step into the IVC accumulators, and checks the result with the decider.


  

  

    
Checks a witness against a circuit compiled by Circom, over BN254. The check runs twice, once directly and once through the conversion to CCS, the form zk-HyperKVM folds.
